🎧 What Is khopesh?

khopesh is a desktop app that takes a normal music file and separates it into two clean pieces: the vocals and the instrumental. Think of it like a magic filter. You give it a finished song, and it hands you back a karaoke version with the singing removed, plus an acapella version with only the singing.

The important part is where this happens. Everything runs on your own machine. Your music files never leave your computer, nothing gets uploaded to a website, and you do not need an internet connection once the app is on your machine. That means your files stay private, the process does not slow down because of a slow connection, and you are not limited by upload sizes or monthly credits.

khopesh is built on modern music separation models that produce remarkably clear results, and it is packaged as a friendly desktop application. You do not need to install Python, run commands, or understand anything about machine learning. If you can drag a file into a window, you can use khopesh.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Windows showed a warning when I opened the app

This is normal for software that Windows has not seen before. Click the option that allows the app to run, and only do so if you downloaded it from the official release page linked on this page.

I cannot find the file I downloaded

Open File Explorer and look in your Downloads folder. You can also press the Windows key and type "Downloads" to jump straight there. If nothing is there, check your browser's download list and try again.

The split is taking a long time

Separation is real work for your computer. A five-minute song can take several minutes on an older laptop. Close other heavy programs, keep the computer plugged in, and leave the app in the foreground until it finishes.

The vocals still have some music in them

Perfect separation is a hard problem, and results vary by song. Dense mixes, heavy reverb, and live recordings are the toughest cases. Try a higher quality version of the same track, since compressed files give the app less to work with.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once the app and its models are on your computer, you can disconnect completely and keep splitting songs. Nothing is sent anywhere.

💡 Tips for Better Results

  • Start with a good source. A high-bitrate file or lossless copy will always beat a low-quality rip.
